Unfollowr Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-01 · Contact: torytekkai@gmail.com

What Unfollowr does

Unfollowr helps you analyze Instagram follower / following relationships on your device (who does not follow you back, list changes between syncs, and related tools).

Data we process

  • On device: follower and following lists, usernames, optional profile photos from Instagram responses or your official JSON export, app settings, and Premium entitlement flags stored with SharedPreferences / secure storage for session tokens.
  • We do not operate a backend that stores your Instagram password. If you use live login, the password is used only to create a session and is not written to disk by Unfollowr.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Third parties

  • Google Play Billing — processes subscription purchases.
  • Google AdMob — shows ads to free users (may use advertising ID subject to your device settings).
  • Instagram / Meta — if you choose live login or export data from Instagram, their policies also apply.

Your choices

You can log out to clear session data on the device, use official Instagram data export instead of password login, and remove the app at any time.

Children

Unfollowr is not directed at children under 13.
